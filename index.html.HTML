About PowerNova

Your trusted partner in power sector infrastructure development

Engineering Excellence in Power Infrastructure

PowerNova International is a Sri Lankan power sector EPC & Supply company delivering comprehensive engineering solutions across civil, electrical, and mechanical disciplines.

We specialize in Solar PV Projects (Rooftop & Ground-Mounted), Transmission Lines & Substations, and MV Installations & Grid Infrastructure. Our end-to-end services span from feasibility through commissioning for local and international markets.

Committed to quality, safety, and sustainability, we reduce technical and execution risks for investors, developers, and utilities through innovative engineering and world-class project execution.
